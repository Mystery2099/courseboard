# CISY 8503

## Self-Review Questions for your Labs

### Site Identity and Purpose

1. **The Needs Assessment**
   a. Is it appropriate for the problem? Why or why not?
   b. Complaints and/or positives
   c. Suggestions for improvement

2. **What is the goal(s) of this software?**
   a. How are these goals communicated?
   b. Are the design, navigation, security and functionality supporting the software’s goals?
   c. Complaints and/or positives
   d. Suggestions for improvement

3. **Who is the target audience?**
   a. Discuss the stated or perceived audience’s demographics
   b. Do the design, navigation, security and functionality appeal to this audience?
   c. Complaints and/or positives
   d. Suggestions for improvement

4. **Does the software communicate the available tasks effectively?**
   a. Are these elements functional?
   b. As a whole, do these elements work together?
   c. Complaints and/or positives
   d. Suggestions for improvement

### Interactions, Content Delivery and Accessibility

5. **How does the software deliver its content? Is the content delivered accessible to a variety of users?**
   a. Does the software provide multiple ways to deliver its material? What are these ways?
   b. Does the software effectively deliver material in an accessible manner?
   c. Complaints and/or positives
   d. Suggestions for improvement

6. **Does the software display and/or deliver its information regarding content, interactions and use effectively?**
   a. Complaints and/or positives
   b. Suggestions for improvement

7. **Do the software’s functional elements work well?**
   a. Do the software’s functional elements “work”? Are these features timely?
   b. Are instructions included or is the software intuitive?
   c. Complaints and/or positives
   d. Suggestions for improvement

### User Interface and User Experience

8. **Is the software interface appropriate for the software’s intended uses and audience?**
   a. Complaints and/or positives
   b. Suggestions for improvement

9. **Are the design layout guidelines being used appropriate to the software’s problem solving approach?**
   a. Complaints and/or positives
   b. Suggestions for improvement

### Software Security

10. **Does the software follow industry specifications regarding the implementation of security practices?**
    a. Complaints and/or positives
    b. Suggestions for improvement

11. **Is the software appropriately secure based on its needs, users and intended purposes?**
    a. Complaints and/or positives
    b. Suggestions for improvement

### Review Pointers

Point out what you didn’t like and what the software did well in a professional manner.

- Do this after your first interaction with the software and again after several interactions to see if you have changed your mind.
- Use constructive terms and if possible direct the individual to resources that may help in the implementation of better software.
- If a design element does not make sense to you immediately or improve the interface, the developer needs to know your opinion. Then look at the software as if you were the intended target audience and share your comments.
- Focus on improving your software, rather than just earning points for CISY 8503.
